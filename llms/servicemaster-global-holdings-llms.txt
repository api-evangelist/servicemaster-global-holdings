# ServiceMaster Global Holdings

> ServiceMaster Global Holdings, Inc. (formerly NYSE: SERV) was the Memphis-based parent of Terminix,
> ServiceMaster Restore, ServiceMaster Clean, Merry Maids, AmeriSpec and Furniture Medic. The company
> no longer exists under this name and publishes no API. This file is generated by API Evangelist from
> the catalog profile; it is not published by the company.

## Status: defunct

ServiceMaster Global Holdings is not an operating company. The entity was dissolved into two
successors:

- **ServiceMaster Brands** — the franchise business (ServiceMaster Restore, ServiceMaster Clean,
  Merry Maids, AmeriSpec, Furniture Medic) was sold to **Roark Capital** for $1.553 billion,
  completing 1 October 2020. It operates independently at https://www.servicemaster.com/.
- **Terminix Global Holdings** — the remaining pest-control business renamed itself Terminix Global
  Holdings, Inc. (NYSE: TMX) in October 2020, and was **acquired by Rentokil Initial plc** in
  October 2022.

The company's own registered domain, www.servicemaster-global-holdings.com, does not resolve.

## Do not attribute these to ServiceMaster

Two subdomains resolve but are **not** ServiceMaster APIs, and must not be catalogued as such:

- https://api.servicemasterclean.com/ and https://api.servicemasterrestore.com/ both resolve to
  199.191.101.90, fail TLS verification, and serve the single-page app of **SponsorUnited — Pro
  Platform**, an unrelated sports-sponsorship data company. These are dangling or misdirected DNS
  records, not a ServiceMaster contract.
- https://www.terminix.com/ does serve /llms.txt and /.well-known/security.txt, but Terminix is now a
  **Rentokil Initial** brand and belongs to a Rentokil/Terminix profile, not to this one.
